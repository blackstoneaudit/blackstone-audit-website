About Us

Blackstone Audit

We are a team of auditors, tax advisors and lawyers helping businesses in Uzbekistan operate transparently, meet regulatory requirements, and grow with confidence.

Our approach

Expertise without juggling separate advisors

We bring audit, tax, legal and accounting expertise together in one team, so clients don't have to piece together a solution from separate consultants.

Every engagement is built on an understanding of the client's industry, international auditing and accounting standards, and hands-on experience working across Uzbekistan and Central Asia.

What sets us apart

  • A multidisciplinary team: audit, tax, legal and accounting under one roof
  • Work in line with international standards (ISA, IFRS) and local legislation
  • A tailored approach for every client, regardless of business size
  • Service available in Russian, English and Uzbek
  • Strict confidentiality and protection of client data
